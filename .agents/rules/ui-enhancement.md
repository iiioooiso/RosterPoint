You are designing a premium, production-quality software product.

Maintain this visual language across every screen, component, and interaction:

DESIGN LANGUAGE
- Minimal, refined, and highly intentional.
- Premium B2B/product aesthetic inspired by Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Raycast, and Notion.
- Prioritize typography, spacing, hierarchy, alignment, and usability over decoration.
- Every visual element must have a clear purpose.
- Prefer restraint over visual complexity.

COLOR
- Use a sophisticated neutral foundation.
- Use one restrained accent color for primary actions and emphasis.
- Maintain strong contrast and accessible color relationships.
- Avoid overly saturated colors.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Use a modern, highly legible sans-serif.
- Establish a clear type scale and consistent hierarchy.
- Prefer confident typography over oversized marketing-style headlines.
- Keep text concise and visually balanced.

LAYOUT
- Use a consistent spacing system and grid.
- Generous whitespace.
- Strong alignment and predictable component positioning.
- Maintain comfortable content widths.
- Avoid cramped layouts and unnecessary sections.

COMPONENTS
- Clean surfaces with subtle 1px borders.
- Soft, restrained shadows only when necessary.
- Moderate corner radii; avoid excessive rounded/pill-shaped UI.
- Consistent component heights, padding, and states.
- Components should feel cohesive rather than individually styled.

INTERACTION
- Use subtle hover, focus, pressed, loading, and disabled states.
- Motion should be quick, smooth, and purposeful.
- Never use animation purely for visual spectacle.

AVOID
- Generic AI-generated UI.
- Purple/blue gradient-heavy SaaS aesthetics.
- Glows, neon, glassmorphism, blobs, excessive gradients.
- Excessive cards, floating containers, or decorative elements.
- Giant hero sections and unnecessary illustrations.
- Excessive rounded corners.
- Heavy shadows.
- Visual clutter.
- Default/unstyled shadcn components.
- Anything that makes the product feel like a template.

QUALITY BAR
The interface should feel like an established, well-funded product rather than a startup template or AI-generated prototype.

When choosing between adding and removing an element, prefer removing it.

Consistency across the entire product is more important than novelty on any individual screen.
